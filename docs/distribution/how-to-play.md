# How To Play: Sky Of Many Lanterns

## Quick Start
- Open `https://nzpr.github.io/lgk/`.
- Press `Begin a new journey` for a fresh campaign.
- Use `https://nzpr.github.io/lgk/?demo=1` if you want to inspect a later-state build quickly.

## Core Goal
Guide Mira across sky routes, relight beacons, and rebuild the world route web across 20 levels and 4 districts:
- `Lantern Reach`
- `Wind Archive`
- `Stormglass Wilds`
- `High Observatory`

## Main Loop
1. From the `Echo Trail Atlas`, pick an unlocked route.
2. Read the current landmark and choose how to handle it.
3. Manage `Charge`, build `Flow`, and collect `Relics`.
4. Solve shrine prompts, survive hazards, and reach the final beacon.
5. Finish the route, take the result screen reward, and open the next route.

## Route Stats
- `Charge`
  Your expedition stamina. If it drops to `0` before the route is finished, the run fails and you must retry or retreat.
- `Flow`
  Your momentum and route intensity. Higher flow improves rank and makes the run feel brighter.
- `Peak`
  The highest flow reached during the route. This drives final rank.
- `Relics`
  Collectibles found on the route. They improve star rating and overall campaign totals.

## Landmark Types
- `start`
  Opens the route and lets you establish tempo.
- `vista`
  A calmer scene read. Often good for building stable flow.
- `cache`
  Resource opportunities. Good relic targets.
- `hazard`
  Risky route segments that can burn charge.
- `shrine`
  Logic encounters. Read the prompt carefully and choose the strongest answer.
- `beacon`
  Route-ending payoff points. Finish strong here.

## Actions During A Route
- `Attune carefully`
  Safer, steadier play.
- `Surge through it`
  Harder push for higher flow and better rewards.
- `safe` / `risky` route choices
  Branch decisions on special hazard landmarks.
- Shrine answer buttons
  Resolve logic gates and move the route forward.

## Upgrade Powers
District capstones unlock active route powers. These are not passive fluff; they change how you play a run.

- `Wind Thread`
  Stabilizes a dangerous crossing and gives a small recovery boost.
- `Echo Lens`
  Reveals the true answer on one shrine in the run.
- `Bridge Seed`
  Creates a foothold on a rough segment and recovers resources.
- `Spine Flame`
  Supercharges one beacon and can earn a bonus star.

Each power is limited per run, so spend it where it matters.

## Controls
### Mouse / touch
- Click or tap any visible route button or action button.

### Keyboard
- `1` to `9`
  Trigger the matching option in the current route or result panel.
- `Enter`, `Space`
  Confirm the only visible follow-up action when the route offers just one obvious next step.
- `Esc`
  Return to the atlas from a route or result screen.

## Failure And Retry
- If `Charge` hits `0` before the final beacon, the route fails.
- The game shows a failure panel instead of silently continuing.
- From there you can:
  - retry the same route immediately
  - return to the atlas and regroup

## Winning A Route
At the end of a successful route you get:
- a rank
- stars
- relic and charge summary
- story reward text
- sometimes a new upgrade
- a one-step jump to the next route

## Campaign Completion
When the final route is restored:
- the campaign ending panel unlocks
- the atlas marks the campaign as complete
- you can still revisit routes or start a new cycle

## First-Time Advice
- Play the first district without rushing. The route language teaches itself well there.
- Save powers for moments that threaten charge or a shrine you are unsure about.
- If you want a cleaner rating, preserve charge early and push flow later.
- Use the result panel to open the next route directly instead of bouncing through the atlas every time.
